PopCheck help

PopCheck shows which bloon types each tower crosspath and hero level can pop, plus this week's boss and race with hints.

Common questions

A result looks wrong. Results come from the game's own attack data for that crosspath. If a patch changed something, email the tower, the crosspath and what you saw in game, and it will be checked.

The Events tab says it couldn't refresh. It needs an internet connection to reach Ninja Kiwi's Open Data service. The last loaded week is kept for when you're offline.

I bought Remove Ads on another phone. Open Support and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID.

Can I get a refund for a tip? Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Contact

Email popcheck.app@gmail.com. One person reads these, usually within a few days.
