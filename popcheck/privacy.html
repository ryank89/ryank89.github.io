PopCheck privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026. Applies to the PopCheck iPhone app and this website.

PopCheck is a free guide made by one developer, Ryan Kuithe. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and the developer does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

What stays on your phone

Your last tower build, your difficulty setting, a count of tips you've given, whether you bought Remove Ads, and a copy of this week's event rules are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Event rules

The Events tab downloads this week's boss and race rules from Ninja Kiwi's public Open Data service (data.ninjakiwi.com). Like any website, that service receives your device's IP address with the request. PopCheck sends nothing else and does not sign you in. See Ninja Kiwi's own privacy policy for how they handle requests.

Ads

The app shows one banner ad from Google AdMob. PopCheck asks Google for non-personalized ads only and never asks for permission to track you across other apps. Google may still collect device information, such as an ad identifier for frequency capping, approximate location from your IP address and ad interactions, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, for example in the UK, Europe and some US states, Google's consent screen appears first, and you can change your choice any time under Support > Privacy choices. Google's practices are described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Buying Remove Ads stops the ad SDK from showing ads.

The website has no ads.

Purchases and tips

Tips and Remove Ads are handled entirely by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details, and only learns that a purchase happened (as anonymous sales totals from Apple).

Children

PopCheck is a general-audience guide and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and its ads are non-personalized and limited to general-audience content.

Your choices and contact

You can reset or limit ads in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and Apple Advertising, change consent under Support > Privacy choices, or delete the app to remove all local data. Questions: popcheck.app@gmail.com.

PopCheck is an unofficial fan guide, not affiliated with or endorsed by Ninja Kiwi.
