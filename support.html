Support

Something broken, confusing or unfair to an elf? Email haul.prowl.2z@icloud.com and tell us your phone model, your OS version and what happened. We read everything and answer in a few days.

Quick answers

How do I bowl?

Drag sideways to move the ball, swipe up to throw, and curve the swipe to hook. The gold hard hat elf is the Steward: flatten him for 5 bonus coins.

How do I turn the swearing off?

Tap Crude in the bar at the bottom so it says Off. The game starts in Clean mode, which has no swearing at all, and Crude can only be switched on after the age check confirms you are 13 or older. Bleep beeps out the worst words while Crude is on.

I can't hear the elves.

Check that Sound is On in the bottom bar, that your phone is not on silent, and that the volume is up. Voices start after your first tap.

What is Advent Lane?

25 doors, each with a different lane, a ball goal and a gift. In December one door opens per day. Before December every door is open as a preview.

What do I get for watching an ad?

Optional videos give you double coins after a game or an extra ball in Advent Lane. Ads never change a Ranked score. You can always say no thanks.

Ranked and the leaderboard

Ranked mode keeps its own best scores. On iPhone, scores go to Game Center. On Android your Ranked bests stay on your phone in this version.

My progress is gone.

Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app, clearing its data or switching phones starts you fresh. There is no account to restore it from.

Remove ads, restore a purchase, refunds

On iPhone, one optional purchase, No Ads ($1.99, one time), removes all ads forever. It is handled by Apple; we never see your card or payment details. There is no purchase on Android at launch. Restore (iPhone only): open Games, tap Restore purchases (use the same Apple ID you bought with). Refunds are decided by Apple, not by us: use Apple's request a refund page. Email us if the purchase does not restore.

Change my ad choices

On iPhone: Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking. On Android: Settings, Privacy, Ads, and delete or reset your advertising ID. In EU, UK and some other regions you can reopen the consent form from the game's settings once it is available there, or write to us.

Is this for kids?

No. It is rated 13+ and asks for your birth year before any ads. If you are under 13, there are no ads and no data is sent to an ad network, but a parent should check the rating before you play.

Delete or ask about your data

We do not keep an account or server record of you. See the privacy policy for what advertisers collect and how to ask them to delete it. Email us for anything else.
