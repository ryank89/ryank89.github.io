Privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026. Applies to the Jingle Pins mobile game and this website.

Jingle Pins is a game from the Jingle Pins developer ("we", "us"). Contact: [CONTACT-EMAIL]. This page explains what the game does with your information. Short version: we do not run accounts or servers about you, we do not sell your data, and the only parties who collect data through the game are the ad network and, on iPhone, Apple.

Who this is for

The game is rated 13+ and is not directed to children under 13. We ask for your birth year on first launch, before any ads. If you say you are under 13, the ad software is never started and nothing is sent to an advertiser. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

What we collect ourselves

  • Nothing about you on our own servers. No account, name, email or location.
  • On your device only: your coins, best scores, Advent progress, toggle settings (Sound, Crude, Bleep), your age band (the age group worked out from the birth year you enter, which is not sent anywhere) and your consent choices. This stays on your phone and is deleted when you delete the app or clear its data.
  • Clips of your game, on your device: the clip button records a short video of the game (its picture and sound) on your phone. Nothing is uploaded by us. A clip is saved or shared only when you tap share and choose where it goes, through your phone's own share sheet.
  • Name tags and party player names, on your device only: names you give elves or party players are stored on your phone and are never sent to us or anyone else. A name that is on screen when you record a clip will appear in that clip, so check it before you share.
  • Door reminders, on your device: if you opt in, the game asks your phone for permission to show notifications and schedules local reminders for the Advent calendar on the phone itself. No server is involved. You can turn them off in the game or in your phone's settings. Reminders are not offered to players under 13.
  • A small error counter, on your device only: the game counts crashes and errors (a number and up to three short messages with web addresses and digits removed) so a problem can be diagnosed. It is not sent to us automatically. If you choose to copy it from the game and send it to us, we use it only to fix the problem.
  • A settings file from our website: when the game starts it may download one small file (ops.json) from this site. It can switch ads off or change how often they appear and carries no personal data. The site host (GitHub) can see your IP address when the file is requested, as with any website. If the download fails the game uses the last copy it saved.
  • This website is a static page with no analytics and no cookies of ours. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may see your IP address when you open it.

Ads: Google AdMob

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. When ads run, Google and its ad partners may collect: your advertising identifier, your IP address and an approximate location derived from it, how you interact with ads (taps, video views), and app and device diagnostics such as crash and performance data. They use it to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve their services. Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive a profile of you from them.

  • Frequency: no ad in your first three games, at least three minutes between full-screen ads, never during a throw. Optional videos are always your choice.
  • 13 to 17: we limit ads to non-personalized and do not ask to track you.
  • Under 13: no ads, no ad software; optional reward offers are simply given free.

Consent (Europe, UK and some other places)

Where the law requires it, the game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads load. You can accept, decline or manage choices, and change your mind later. If you decline, ads that do not use personal data may still appear.

Tracking on iPhone (App Tracking Transparency)

On iPhone we ask once, after your first game, whether the game may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites for advertising. If you tap "Ask App Not to Track" the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are less personalized. You can change this any time in Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking.

Purchases

The game has one optional in-app purchase, No Ads ($1.99). On iPhone, payment is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; there is no purchase on Android at launch. We never see your card or payment details, and we keep no purchase records or accounts. The game only learns from the store whether this purchase is owned, and keeps that fact on your device. You can restore it from the Games screen on iPhone using the same Apple ID. Refunds are handled by Apple. If you have bought No Ads, the game shows no ads and starts no ad software.

Leaderboard and Ranked

On iPhone, Ranked scores can be posted to Apple Game Center, which is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Your Game Center name and score appear to other Game Center players per your Game Center settings. We do not receive your Apple ID. On Android in this version, scores stay on your phone. The web edition of the game, if you play it in a browser, keeps scores in your browser and, if its host provides a shared leaderboard, shows names from that host.

How long we keep data

We keep nothing on our servers. Advertisers keep data under their own policies. Local game data lasts until you delete the app.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with anyone except as described above (the ad network, and Apple for Game Center). We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings; opt out of personalized ads in Google's Ads Settings.
  • EU and UK: you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, objection and portability. Since we hold no data about you, the ad network is the party to ask; you can also write to us and we will help route the request.
  • California and other US states: you may have rights to know, delete, correct and opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information. Sharing with the ad network for advertising may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" in some states; you can opt out by declining consent or tracking in the game or in your phone settings, or by emailing us.
  • Texas residents: Texas law (SB 2420, the App Store Accountability Act) has the app stores verify age and get a parent's consent for minors. If your store sends us an age category or consent signal, we use it only to follow that law and to limit features, never for ads or marketing, never share it, and do not keep it after use. Texas residents also have the privacy rights above under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, which we will honor on request.

Security

Ad data is sent over encrypted connections. No method is perfectly secure.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, if needed, ask again in the game.
