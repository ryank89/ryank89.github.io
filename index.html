What you get

  • Quick Game and Classic. Five frames in about three minutes, or the full ten.
  • Advent Lane. 25 doors, each with a new lane twist, a goal and a gift. One opens a day in December.
  • Four elves, four voices. Bench the ones who annoy you. They will notice.
  • Ranked mode. A score that counts, on iPhone with Game Center.
  • Real mess. Wobbles that turn into delayed falls, kickbacks, slow motion.

Your lane, your language

The game starts Clean, with no swearing at all. Once the age check confirms you are 13 or older you can switch Crude on from the bar at the bottom: real swearing (crap, hell, ass, the s-word and the b-word; never the f-word or a slur) and toilet jokes. Bleep covers the swear words. It is a game for teens and up.

The honest part

The game is free because of ads. Some are optional videos for bonus coins, others come after a game, never in your first three games and never mid-throw. We ask for your consent where the law says to, and on iPhone we ask before tracking. Details are in the privacy policy. Trouble? Visit support.
